Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: So its easier to do group projects, where each person has different roles and tasks and doing it all in one file can by messy 
and disorganized so we make branches where each person can do their own work. In addition, allows us to track our own and each 
other progress and revert back to any only changes if we reach a roadblock or problem.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: Data Science could be helpful for Meteorology. An example is to use data from past history 
on weather patterns in specific locations which could be used to create models to predict the
weather in the future based on specific conditions.


Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: The difference is that merging takes two branches and combines them into one while still 
referring to past histories of both branches even after joining, whereas rebasing which still acounts 
in history of both branches but it organizes history as one rather than two separate branches.
